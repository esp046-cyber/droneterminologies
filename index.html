🚁 Drone Beginner's Guide

Essential Terminology for New Pilots

📦 Basic Components

Drone/Aircraft
The flying device itself with propellers and motors
Remote Control/Controller
The handheld device you use to control the drone
Propellers
The spinning blades that make the drone fly
Battery
Power source for the drone (rechargeable via USB, ~40 min charge time). Battery light indicates charging status - light turns off when full
Fuselage
The main body of the drone. Can offset in different directions during flight requiring trim adjustments
USB Charging Cable
Cable used to charge the drone battery - connects to computer or wall adapter
Battery Charging Socket
Port on the drone battery where charging cable connects
Configurator
Additional device/adapter sold separately for advanced drone settings

🎮 Control Terms

Throttle
Left stick up/down - controls altitude (height). Push up = ascend, push down = descend. Also controls left/right rotation (yaw)
Direction Stick
Right stick - controls which way the drone moves (forward/backward/left/right fly)
Trim Buttons
Small adjustment buttons around the sticks to correct drift during flight. Four buttons: Left, Right, Forward, Backward (Afterword) fine-tuning
Speed Grade
Setting to control how fast the drone responds to your commands (adjustable speed levels)
Roll
Tilting left or right rotation movement - One-touch roll button available
Yaw
Spinning/rotating the drone in place (left or right rotation)
Left/Right Turn
Throttle stick controls for rotating the drone left or right while hovering
Forward/Backward Control
Direction stick movement that makes drone fly toward or away from you

✈️ Flight Operations

Takeoff
Getting the drone airborne from the ground
Landing
Bringing the drone back down to the ground
Hover
Keeping the drone steady in one position in the air
Drift
When the drone slowly moves in a direction without you controlling it
One-Touch Takeoff
Press a button and drone automatically takes off
One-Touch Landing
Press a button and drone automatically lands
Emergency Stop
Long-press button to immediately stop all motors (drone will fall!)
Auto Launch/Land
Automatic takeoff and landing feature controlled from app interface
Ascend/Descend
Moving the drone up (ascending) or down (descending) using throttle
Joystick Maneuver
Directional stick movements that control the vehicle's position

🎯 Flight Modes

Headless Mode
Control mode where forward on the stick always moves drone away from you, regardless of which way the drone is facing. Activated by pressing headless mode button - remote beeps to confirm. Aircraft front is defaulted to front of remote position in this mode
Normal Mode
Standard control where forward moves the front of the drone forward (you need to track which way is "front")

🔧 Setup & Maintenance

Calibration
Process to reset the drone's sensors so it flies straight and level
Frequency Pairing
Connecting the remote control to the drone wirelessly - must align automatically when both are powered on
Trim Calibration
Fine-tuning adjustments during flight to stop drifting
Low Power Alarm
Warning when battery is below 50% (land immediately)
Line of Sight
Keeping the drone where you can see it while flying
One-Key Calibration
Single button press to automatically calibrate the drone sensors
Former Fine-Tuning
Additional trim adjustment button for precise control

⚠️ Safety Terms

Collision
When drone hits something (wall, tree, person, etc.). Can cause impact or external collision requiring recalibration
Deviation
When drone doesn't fly straight after a crash (needs calibration to correct direction)
Polarity
Correct +/- direction for installing batteries. CRITICAL: Wrong polarity damages drone. Do not mix old and new or different types of batteries
Flat Surface / Horizontal Surface
Level ground needed for calibration, safe takeoff/landing, and frequency pairing
Manipulator/Guide
The person operating the remote control - children should be guided by an adult
Stationary State
Drone must be landed and not flying when switching between remote and app control
Craft is Fully Powered
Remote control throttle should be pushed up slowly when flying for the first time to ensure proper power

📱 App/Advanced Features

APP Control
Flying the drone using a smartphone app instead of remote. Note: Cannot control takeoff/landing simultaneously with remote
Controller ON/OFF
Switch between remote control and app control modes (drone must be stationary when switching)
VR Mode
First-person view through phone/goggles
Flight Path
Pre-programmed route for the drone to follow automatically
Gesture Control
Using hand motions to control the drone via app
Photo/Video Mode
Camera functions - switch between photo and video recording
Flip Screen
Automatic flip/roll trick feature
Zoom Screen
Camera zoom function in the app interface
Follow Me
Drone automatically follows the controller/phone GPS location
Motion Sensitive Piloting
Control drone by tilting your phone
More Functions Menu
Additional settings and features in app interface
Video Edits/Music/Filters
Post-flight editing tools in the app
Album/Back
Access saved photos and videos, navigate app menus
Landscape/Portrait Mode
Camera orientation settings

📏 Important Measurements

2-3 Meters
Safe minimum distance from people during flight
50% Battery
Critical low power threshold (land when you reach this)
40 Minutes
Full charging time for drone battery
AAA Batteries
What the remote control uses (requires separate purchase)

Screwdriver Set
Small precision screwdrivers (Phillips and flathead) for opening battery compartments and accessing internal components
Hex Key / Allen Wrench
Small hexagonal tools for tightening propeller mounts and motor screws
Propeller Removal Tool
Special tool or wrench for safely removing and installing propellers
Multimeter
Device to test battery voltage, check electrical connections, and diagnose power issues
Soldering Iron
Tool for repairing broken wire connections, battery terminals, or motor wires
Tweezers
Precision tweezers for handling small parts, wires, and connectors
Wire Cutters/Strippers
Tools for cutting and stripping insulation from replacement wires
Hot Glue Gun
Used to secure loose components, reinforce connections, or repair broken body parts
Spare Propellers
Replacement blades for damaged or broken propellers (most common repair part)
Spare Motors
Replacement motors when original motors burn out or fail
Spare Landing Gear
Replacement legs/feet that protect drone during landing
Battery Tester
Device to check battery health, capacity, and charging cycles
Balance Charger
Advanced charger that ensures all battery cells charge evenly for optimal performance
Thread Lock / Loctite
Adhesive to prevent screws from loosening due to vibration during flight
Cleaning Brush
Soft brush for removing dust and debris from motors and electronics
Isopropyl Alcohol
Cleaning solution for circuit boards and electronic components (70-90% concentration)
Zip Ties / Cable Ties
For securing loose wires and organizing cable management
Heat Shrink Tubing
Protective covering for soldered wire connections
Calipers
Precision measuring tool for checking propeller sizes and component dimensions
Motor Puller
Tool for safely removing stuck or seized motors from motor mounts
ESC (Electronic Speed Controller)
Component that controls motor speed - may need replacement if motors don't respond
Flight Controller Board
Main computer board - the "brain" of the drone that processes commands
Gyroscope/Accelerometer
Sensors that help drone maintain stability - part of flight controller
Antenna
Wireless communication component for remote control signal
Receiver Module
Component that receives signals from the remote control
Voltage Regulator
Component that maintains correct voltage to electronics
LED Lights
Indicator lights showing drone status, orientation, and power
Camera Module
Removable/replaceable camera component if equipped
Gimbal
Stabilization mount for camera (if equipped) - keeps video smooth

🥽 FPV Drone Terminology

FPV (First Person View)
Flying the drone while viewing live video feed from drone's camera, as if you're sitting in cockpit
FPV Goggles
Head-mounted display that shows live video feed from drone camera
VTX (Video Transmitter)
Device on drone that transmits video signal to goggles or monitor
VRX (Video Receiver)
Receiver in goggles that picks up video signal from drone
OSD (On-Screen Display)
Information overlay on video feed showing battery voltage, flight time, altitude, speed, etc.
Analog FPV
Traditional FPV system with lower latency but lower video quality (5.8GHz standard)
Digital FPV (DJI/HDZero)
High-definition FPV system with crystal clear video but slightly higher latency
Latency
Delay between drone camera capturing image and you seeing it in goggles (measured in milliseconds)
DVR (Digital Video Recorder)
Built-in recording in goggles that saves FPV footage
HD Recording / Action Cam
Separate high-quality camera (GoPro, Insta360) mounted on drone for final footage
Acro Mode / Rate Mode
Advanced flight mode with no self-leveling - full manual control for flips and tricks
Angle Mode / Stabilized Mode
Beginner mode that auto-levels the drone when sticks are centered
Horizon Mode
Hybrid mode - self-levels but allows flips when sticks pushed to extreme
Betaflight / INAV
Flight controller software/firmware used to configure and tune FPV drones
PID Tuning
Adjusting Proportional, Integral, Derivative values to control flight smoothness and responsiveness
Rates
Settings that control how fast drone rotates when you move sticks
Expo (Exponential)
Stick response curve - makes center stick movements smoother, extremes more aggressive
Quad / Quadcopter
Drone with 4 motors and propellers (most common FPV configuration)
Freestyle
FPV flying style focused on tricks, flips, rolls, and creative maneuvers
Racing
High-speed FPV competition through obstacle courses and gates
Cinematic / Long Range
Smooth, stable FPV flying for video production or distance flights
Whoops / Tiny Whoop
Small indoor FPV drone with prop guards, safe for indoor flying
5-inch / 3-inch Quad
Size classification based on propeller diameter (5" is standard freestyle size)
Frame
Carbon fiber or plastic structure that holds all drone components
Stack
Layered mounting of flight controller and ESC boards
AIO (All-In-One)
Flight controller with built-in ESC, VTX, or receiver - fewer components
4-in-1 ESC
Single board that controls all 4 motors (vs individual ESCs)
BLHeli / ESC Firmware
Software running on ESCs that controls motor behavior
Motor KV Rating
Motor speed constant (RPM per volt) - higher KV = faster spin, lower KV = more torque
Brushless Motor
High-performance motors used in FPV drones (vs brushed motors in toy drones)
LiPo Battery (Lithium Polymer)
High-discharge rechargeable battery used in FPV drones
Battery Cells (1S, 2S, 3S, 4S, 6S)
Number of battery cells - higher number = more voltage and power (1S=3.7V, 4S=14.8V, 6S=22.2V)
mAh (Milliamp Hours)
Battery capacity - higher mAh = longer flight time but heavier
C Rating
Battery discharge rate - how much current battery can safely deliver
XT60 / XT30 Connector
Standard power connectors for FPV batteries
JST Connector
Small connector for tiny drone batteries
Storage Voltage
Safe voltage to store LiPo batteries (3.8V per cell) to extend battery life
LiPo Bag
Fire-resistant bag for safe LiPo battery storage and charging
Smoke Stopper
Safety device that prevents damage from short circuits during testing
Bind / Binding
Pairing transmitter (remote) with receiver on drone
Failsafe
Safety feature that lands/disarms drone if signal is lost
Arming
Activating motors for flight (typically throttle down + yaw right)
Disarming
Deactivating motors (throttle down + yaw left)
Blackbox
Flight data logging for analyzing performance and troubleshooting
Antenna Polarization (LHCP/RHCP)
Direction of video signal spiral - Left or Right Hand Circular Polarization (must match on TX and RX)
Patch Antenna
Directional antenna for long-range video reception
Cloverleaf / Pagoda Antenna
Omnidirectional antennas for FPV video (common on drones and goggles)
Diversity Receiver
System with multiple antennas that automatically picks strongest signal
Rapidfire / True-D
Aftermarket diversity receiver modules for better video reception
Props In / Props Out
Propeller rotation direction configuration affecting flight characteristics
Turtle Mode
Feature that lets you flip drone right-side-up after crashing upside down
Air Mode
Keeps full control authority even at zero throttle for advanced maneuvers
Punch Out
Rapid vertical acceleration from hover or low speed
Power Loop
Vertical loop maneuver going up and over backward

Juicy Flick / Rubik's Cube
Advanced freestyle tricks combining multiple rotations
Blackout / Brownout
Video signal loss (blackout = complete loss, brownout = interference/static)
LOS (Line of Sight)
Flying while watching the drone directly with your eyes (opposite of FPV)
BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight)
Flying too far to see drone with naked eye - often illegal without permit
GPS / Return to Home (RTH)
GPS module that automatically brings drone back to launch point if signal lost

💡 Beginner Tips Summary

  • Always do calibration operation BEFORE taking off for first time
  • Keep small parts away from children - choking hazard
  • Maintain 2-3 meter distance from people during flight
  • Land when battery hits 50% - cannot fly when power is low
  • Beginners should use remote control (apps are more difficult)
  • Practice hovering before moving around
  • Remove batteries when not in use from both drone and remote
  • Fully charge drone battery before first use (40 minutes)
  • Turn on drone first, then remote control connection
  • Push throttle up slowly on first flight
  • Don't mix old/new or different battery types
  • Use headless mode for easier beginner control
  • Remote control requires 3 AAA batteries (sold separately)
  • Always land drone before switching between remote and app control
  • Avoid hitting people's heads, faces, or bodies when flying
